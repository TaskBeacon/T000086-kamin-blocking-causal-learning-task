# Task Plot Brief

Title: Kamin Blocking and Causal Learning Task
Construct: causal learning / cue competition

## Participant-visible flow

- Overall: Chinese food-allergy prediction task followed by individual causal ratings.
- Single-food learning: one bordered food card, three-choice prediction, then green/red outcome box, then blank ITI.
- Additivity demonstration: two food cards, the same prediction choices, then a double red “strong allergic reaction” outcome box.
- Blocking/control compound learning: two food cards (AB or CD), prediction, ordinary red allergic-reaction feedback, then blank ITI.
- Causal test: one food card, 0–8 likelihood rating, no feedback, then blank ITI.

## Exact visible labels

- Heading: `患者这顿饭吃了：`
- Foods used as representative examples: `酸奶`, `蘑菇`, `胡萝卜`, `葡萄`, `奶酪`, `火腿`
- Prediction choices: `1 无过敏反应`, `2 过敏反应`, `3 强烈过敏反应`
- Outcomes: `无过敏反应`, `过敏反应`, `强烈过敏反应`
- Test scale: `0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8`; anchors `肯定不会`, `可能会`, `肯定会`

## Timing

- Prediction: 10 s maximum.
- Feedback: 2 s.
- Causal rating: 20 s maximum.
- ITI: 0.5 s.

1. Single-food learning (e.g. A+ / K−).

3. Blocking/control compounds (AB+ / CD+).
